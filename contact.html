Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I receive my order?

Most orders are delivered within 24 to 72 hours depending on your location and the product category. Some bulky or custom items may take longer.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking update and delivery confirmation by SMS or email.

Do you support returns and refunds?

Yes, we accept returns on eligible items within 7 days of delivery. Refunds are processed once the returned product is verified.

How do I contact a seller?

Use the contact form above or reach out via the seller information shown on the product page to ask about stock, pricing, and delivery.

Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes. Business and reseller customers can contact our sales team for discounted bulk pricing and delivery arrangements.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept mobile money, bank transfer, cash on delivery for eligible locations, and selected online payment options.
