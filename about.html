About Wakibi Market

Your trusted online marketplace for quality products across Uganda

Our Story

Wakibi Market was built to make shopping easier, smarter, and more local. We connect buyers with trusted sellers offering everything from electronics and fashion to home essentials, vehicles, and everyday household goods.

Our mission is simple: help people discover products they need and support sellers who are building businesses across Uganda. We created a platform that brings convenience, trust, and variety into one online shopping experience.

Our Mission

We aim to make e-commerce accessible, reliable, and rewarding for both customers and sellers. Our mission is to:

  • ✓ Offer a wide range of products for everyday life and business needs
  • ✓ Support local businesses and entrepreneurs across Uganda
  • ✓ Create a safe and convenient online shopping experience
  • ✓ Deliver excellent customer service before, during, and after purchase
  • ✓ Make product discovery easy through clear categories and trusted listings
